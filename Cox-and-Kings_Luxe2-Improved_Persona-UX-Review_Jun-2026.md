# Cox & Kings - "Luxe 2 - Improved" Persona UX Review

**Scope:** This review covers **only the `/luxe2-improved` variant** of the homepage. It does **not** assess `/luxe2` or any other page.

**Date:** June 2026
**Method:** Four user-testing personas independently reviewed the full `/luxe2-improved` page (desktop + mobile screenshots, top to bottom). Each scored eight UX dimensions out of 10 and listed advantages (with reasons) and pain points. Scores and verbatim critiques follow.

---

## The review panel

| Persona | Who they are | What they came to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Ananya Reddy** | 33, Hyderabad marketing entrepreneur; design-literate, Instagram-inspired | Find a tailor-made anniversary / honeymoon-style trip (Japan or Italy) - *not* a package |
| **Karan Shah** | 29, Mumbai professional; mobile-first, high-intent, impatient | Compare a destination-decided trip against MakeMyTrip, Thomas Cook, SOTC, Thrillophilia |
| **Rajesh Menon** | 44, Bengaluru IT director | Book a premium escorted Europe group tour for parents (68 & 65), wife and two kids |
| **Sunita Agarwal** | 58, Delhi homemaker; cautious, modest with technology | Verify the brand she remembers from decades ago is genuinely back and safe before trusting it |

---

## Executive summary

The panel's average overall score is **7.0 / 10**. All four personas agreed the page **looks and feels genuinely premium and trustworthy** - the strongest, most consistent praise was for heritage/trust signalling, editorial visual design, and believable social proof (real named Indian families). The brand "earning trust" job is largely done.

The shared weaknesses are about **conversion clarity, not aesthetics**:

1. **The Escorted Group Tours product is hidden and never pays off** - the single biggest cross-persona issue. The group option is a secondary, greyed toggle, and selecting it surfaces no actual group departure (no date, no seats, no all-in price). The two buyers who *need* it (Rajesh, Sunita) and the comparer (Karan) all stalled here.
2. **Pricing clarity is the lowest-scoring dimension (5.8 avg).** Per-person luxury prices marked "excl. international flights" confuse the comparer and group buyers, while the group card promises "flights included" but no such priced product is shown.
3. **A mobile bug hurt everyone:** the floating "Call an Expert / WhatsApp" action bar overlaps card content. **All four personas flagged it.**
4. **A genuine segment tension on pricing:** Karan wants *more* price transparency (all-in numbers to compare); Ananya wants *less* hard pricing (fixed "FROM ₹X PP" tags undercut the bespoke "we design it around you" promise). This argues for treating the two products differently rather than identically.

## What works - and why

1. **Heritage & trust scaffolding (cited by all four).** "Since 1758 · 267 years," Condé Nast 2024, "financially protected," "98% would travel again," 4.8 from 14,200 travellers. *Why it works:* it de-risks a high-value, emotional purchase up front. Sunita reads it as proof the brand is genuinely back; Rajesh says it calms a ₹15-lakh decision; Karan calls it "instant respect"; Ananya "leaned in" at the confident hero.

2. **Premium editorial visual design (panel's top score, 8.5).** Serif display type, cinematic real photography, restrained palette, generous whitespace. *Why it works:* it signals "not a discount OTA," which justifies a premium before any number is seen. Karan: "beats MakeMyTrip easily."

3. **Believable, relatable social proof (8.3).** Named Indian travellers with specific quotes - Mr & Mrs Iyer (both 67, Europe group tour), the Nair Family (3 generations, Switzerland), real faces, plus a press-logo row. *Why it works:* it reads as real, not casted. The group/senior buyers literally saw "my people" doing the exact trip they want.

4. **Human / concierge access everywhere.** Call, WhatsApp, Request a callback, a named travel designer (Aanya Kapoor - 12 years, photo, rating), "9am–9pm IST." *Why it works:* at this price, people want to talk to a human before paying. Sunita (trusts a voice over a form) and Rajesh (won't pay before speaking to someone) both rate Conversion highly because of it.

5. **The "Two ways to travel" framing.** Private & Bespoke vs Escorted Group Tours, surfaced as the core decision. *Why it works:* it mirrors the actual choice the user is trying to make instead of burying it. Ananya valued the bespoke-first split that structurally answers her "don't sell me a package" anxiety.

6. **Visible per-person prices on cards (no form to see a number).** *Why it works (for Karan especially):* "I don't have to surrender my phone number to see a price." That transparency is rare among comparators and earns immediate respect.

---

## Pain points - and who flagged them

1. **[P0] Escorted Group Tours is a buried toggle with no payoff** - *Rajesh, Sunita, Karan.* The group option is secondary/greyed; selecting it shows only bespoke luxury cards (Amankora, Aman Venice, Singita, Soneva), never an actual group departure with a date, seat count, or all-in price. The two buyers who need this product cannot find a single bookable group tour.

2. **[P0] Pricing clarity - "excl. international flights" + luxury per-person prices** - *Karan, Rajesh, Sunita* (lowest dimension, 5.8). Karan compares all-in numbers on MMT and distrusts a "from ₹2.4L excl. flights"; Rajesh notes the group card promises "flights included" yet every priced card excludes them; Sunita finds the prices past budget and fears surprises.

3. **[P0] Mobile floating CTA bar overlaps card content** - *all four personas.* The sticky "Call an Expert / WhatsApp" bar covers the exact card text users are trying to read. A clean, quick fix that lifts the lowest-scoring experience dimension.

4. **[P1] Search / findability doesn't deliver intent** - *Karan, Ananya.* Karan searched "Japan + flights" and got Bhutan/Venice/Maldives plus a long poetic scroll before any bookable tour; Ananya finds the "Where to / How many people / When" search bar tonally like a flights widget, off-key for tailor-made.

5. **[P1] No mention of vegetarian/Indian food or senior pace & comfort** - *Rajesh, Sunita.* "Great for families & seniors" is asserted but never backed (no pace rating, group size, "lifts not stairs," or a dietary line). For senior/family group trips this is close to a dealbreaker.

6. **[P2] Hard price tags vs the bespoke promise** - *Ananya.* "FROM ₹X PP" plus "ENQUIRE" repeated down the page contradict the "every trip is a starting point we reshape" message and invite padded-quote suspicion. *(Note the tension with Karan below.)*

7. **[P2] Low-contrast small grey text** - *Rajesh, Ananya* (minor for Sunita, Karan). The light-grey "excl. international flights" and muted review sub-labels are hard on older eyes.

---

## The key tension to resolve

**Karan wants more pricing transparency; Ananya wants less.** Karan (comparison shopper) needs clear all-in numbers to stop opening competitor tabs. Ananya (bespoke buyer) feels fixed "FROM ₹X PP" tags cheapen a service that's supposed to be "designed around you."

These are not contradictory if the two products are treated differently:
- **Fixed / Group product** → show clear, all-in (flights-included) per-person prices with dates and seats (serves Karan, Rajesh, Sunita).
- **Bespoke product** → soften to "designed from…" / let the number emerge in conversation with the designer (serves Ananya).

---

## Prioritised recommendations

**P0 - fix first (shared, high impact)**
- Give **Escorted Group Tours** an equal front door: when selected, show a real Europe/Switzerland group departure card with a **fixed date, "X of 24 seats left," and an all-in per-person price including flights**, plus a pace rating and typical group size.
- Fix the **mobile floating action bar** so it never overlaps card content (add safe-area bottom padding to the cards / scroll container).
- Clarify pricing: make **flights-included** explicit on the group product and state plainly what bespoke "from" prices exclude.

**P1 - strengthen conversion**
- Make the hero search **deliver intent** - tapping "Japan" should drop the user into Japan results (a group option and a private option) rather than a long brand scroll.
- Add a single honest line near the group product on **vegetarian/Indian meals and senior comfort/pace**.

**P2 - polish**
- Differentiate pricing tone by product (all-in on fixed/group; "designed from" on bespoke).
- Darken low-contrast grey microcopy (the "excl. flights" line, review sub-labels) for readability.

## Appendix - method & notes

- **Variant tested:** `/luxe2-improved` only (desktop 1440px + mobile 390px full-page screenshots, captured June 2026).
- **Personas:** four standing user-testing personas (bespoke buyer, price comparer, premium group-tour buyer, legacy/trust checker), each reviewing independently and in character.
- **Scoring:** each persona rated eight UX dimensions out of 10; the panel average is the unweighted mean of the four scores.
- **A note on the toggle finding:** every persona who selected "Escorted Group Tours" reported seeing only bespoke cards. This is the page's single most repeated, highest-impact issue and should be validated and addressed first.
