Karan reviewed the `/new` composite landing page (localhost:5173/new, `src/pages/New.jsx`) on 2026-07-07. This page stitches together the strongest sections from three prior prototypes: nav/hero/intro/fork/destinations/clips from /improved, heritage/reviews/press/Enaya from /luxe2-improved, and the "Relaxed pace" journey shelf from /journeys.

**Overall: cautious stay, would tap WhatsApp "Enquire Now."** Scores — Answers search 5, Price/value 6, Mobile 7, Proof 8, Speed-to-next-step 8.

**What won him (keep):**
- Prices visible with no form wall — destination grid "Japan from ₹2,60,000/person", journey shelf cards all carry "from ₹X". This is the #1 reason he stays vs SOTC/Thomas Cook.
- Journey cards scannable: nights, season, pace, star rating, View Itinerary + WhatsApp Enquire.
- WhatsApp everywhere + mobile thumb bar = frictionless low-commitment next step.
- Proof strong: 4.9 with Google 4.8 / Tripadvisor 4.9 breakdown, trip-specific reviews, reels.
- Group-vs-custom split clear ("max 18, tour manager" vs "named specialist").

**Recurring gripes (same as journey-landing / improved-homepage reviews — still unfixed):**
1. Hero answers a mood ("More than a destination, it's a journey"), not his search. No destination/price/flights above the fold — has to scroll 2 sections to first Japan price. Biggest bounce risk.
2. "Flights included?" never answered on price cards — his first question, and reason a competitor beats him.
3. Price inconsistency across sections: Japan is ₹2,60,000 (dest grid) vs ₹4,20,000 (journey shelf luxe) vs ₹3,10,000 (bespoke sample) with no label explaining they're different trips — reads like the number moves, trust ding.
4. No single "why pay more than MakeMyTrip" panel — value points (max-18, specialist, 24hr reply, WhatsApp, 1758) are scattered across 5 sections.
5. Too much brand-mood real estate (scatter intro, 260-yrs heritage, counters, press) before tours; long thumb-scroll on mobile.
6. Heritage copy leans on distrust-triggering adjectives ("each journey is a masterpiece") instead of specifics (hotel class, group size, flights).

**Why:** Same persona priorities as [[journey-landing-verdict]] and [[improved-homepage-verdict]] — the /new composite inherited their unfixed hero-price/flights/value-panel gaps.
**How to apply:** If iterating on /new, the highest-leverage fixes are (a) a concrete hero answer with price + flights status, (b) flights in/out label on every price card, (c) reconcile or label the divergent Japan prices, (d) one consolidated vs-competition value box.
