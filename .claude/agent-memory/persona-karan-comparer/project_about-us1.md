Karan reviewed `/about-us1` (localhost:5001) on 2026-07-13, arriving from the Google query "is cox and kings legit now" on mobile. Scored **6.5/10**.

**What worked (keep this, do not water it down):** the hero names the 2020 insolvency in the second sentence and states the 2024 buyout. Naming your own failure above the fold is the single most credible thing across every Cox & Kings page reviewed so far — it beat the LIVE site, /improved, /new and /luxe2-improved on trust. The "Four things to check. Not only with us." section (pay the company not a person, pay by card, ask where the deposit is held) reads as genuinely useful, not marketing.

**Measured facts:**
- Mobile page = 16,318px = **19.3 phone screens**, 1,750 words (~8 min read).
- Timeline = 14 entries, 4.8 screens on mobile; on desktop it is 8,220px = **9.1 screens / 46% of total page scroll**, ~100 wheel notches to clear. "Skip the history" button is sticky and 188x44 (correctly sized) — that button is the only thing making the pin survivable.
- **Header renders TWO overlapping logos**: `cox-logo-new.png` (48x30 at 20,22) and `cox-crest-new.png` (46x29 at 20,0). Visible bug in the first thing on screen.
- Those two logo PNGs are **763KB + 461KB = 1.2MB** for two ~48x30 marks. Real shipped weight, not a dev artifact.
- Hero trust chips ("Owned by Wilson & Hughes since 2024" etc.) are 40px tall — under the 44px tap minimum.

**The core gap:** the page has **zero customer reviews, zero ratings, and the string "2025" appears nowhere on it.** The #proof section is trade-body badges only (IATA/ASTA/USTOA/ATTA/TAAI). Specialists (Meera/Arjun/Nisha) are initials avatars, not photographs. So the page argues the company is *legally* clean but never shows it has *actually run a trip* since the 2024 relaunch. That is the bounce point for a comparer.

**Why:** Karan's search intent is "is cox and kings legit now" — a live-ness question, not a corporate-history question. Company age is what the *old* Cox & Kings had when it collapsed, so 268 years of ledger entries argues against the point.

**How to apply:** Prices are correctly absent from an About page — do not add them. What to add instead: a post-relaunch evidence block (trips run since 2024, travellers carried, a dated recent review with a name and month, real specialist photos), collapse the mobile timeline to ~5 entries behind a "See all 268 years" expander, and fix the doubled logo + compress the logo PNGs.
