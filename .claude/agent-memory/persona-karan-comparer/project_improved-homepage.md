Reviewed the `/improved` homepage prototype (desktop 1440 + mobile 390 screenshots) as Karan on 2026-06.

Verdict: 7/10. Strong content/trust, but price-shy hero and a visible mobile bug.

**What landed well (keep):**
- "Signature Journeys" inclusion grid (flights ex-Mumbai, 4★/5★ central, Max 18 group, included vs not-included) — best section, beats OTAs.
- Curation card: named specialist ("Curated by Meera Sundaram · 18 years"), price + duration + departure months. Real differentiator vs faceless OTA.
- Trust block: IATA/TAAI/ASTA, 20% deposit, free cancellation 7 days, "visa refused = rebook/refund land portion."
- Reviews: 4.7 / Google 4.8 / Tripadvisor 4.9 / 2,400+ verified with trip-tagged quotes.
- Group-vs-custom split is clear ("How would you like to travel?").

**Issues to recheck (these drove the score down):**
1. No price above the fold — hero leads with brand poem, not "Japan from ₹X". Karan searched destination+price.
2. Mobile "Specialists" section visibly BROKEN — overlapping headline text + a blue nav bar dumped on top of the image. Worst single thing; reads as "site is broken."
3. Number repetition — ₹2,45,000 used for BOTH Switzerland destination tile AND the 13-day Grand Switzerland & Italy tour. Looks copy-pasted, erodes trust in the numbers.
4. Brand-fluff creep — "a quarter-millennium of judgment", heritage essay repeated; and Japan (the searched destination) only gets a tile, not a finished example.

**Why:** Karan is mobile-first, price-comparing, bails fast. **How to apply:** if a future revision is shared, confirm hero now shows a destination price, the mobile Specialists overlap is fixed, destination prices are distinct, and a finished Japan example exists.
