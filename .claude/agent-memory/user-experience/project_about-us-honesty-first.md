The `/about-us` page for Cox & Kings India is built honesty-first: the "what happened in 2019–20" section sits **above** the GSAP ScrollTrigger history timeline, not inside it.

**Why:** The insolvency is the single question the trust-checking persona (Sunita, 58, Delhi) arrives with. Putting it behind a 15-milestone scroll experience reads as evasion and confirms her suspicion that the brand is "a slogan borrowed from a famous old name." Naming it in the hero and answering it third turns the timeline from a hiding place into proof — the user already knows the ending, so 1758→2024 becomes pleasurable rather than dreaded.

**How to apply:** Any future edit that demotes, softens, or relocates the "what happened" section below the timeline is a regression. Euphemisms ("restructuring", "challenges", "a difficult chapter") are banned — the copy names the default, the NCLT insolvency, the cancelled trips and the customers who were not made whole.

**Hard rule on money claims:** Every financial-protection statement on this page (client trust account, insolvency cover, refund windows, escrow) is a legal commitment, not marketing. None ships without written Finance/Legal sign-off. Spec placeholders are marked `[VERIFY]` and must stay unshipped rather than be filled with plausible-sounding text. A false protection claim on the page that exists to rebuild trust is the worst possible failure mode.
