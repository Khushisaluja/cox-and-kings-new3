Reviewed the LIVE production site https://coxandkings.com/ on 2026-06-30 as Ananya (bespoke Japan/Italy anniversary buyer). Overall 6/10.

**Why:** This is the real shipped brand, distinct from the localhost prototypes (/luxe2-improved, /improved, /landing) I've reviewed before — useful as the baseline the prototypes are trying to beat.

**How to apply:** When comparing prototype homepages to "the real thing," this is what the live site actually does.

What won me (keep):
- Copy has genuine taste. Homepage hero "Every great journey begins with fine company." Japan hero "The feeling of Japan is silence, sound, and those sudden blooms." — editorial, not SEO. Best asset.
- Soft CTAs ("Explore Now", "View Trip", "Talk to an Expert") instead of ENQUIRE-spam. Reads atelier not OTA.
- /private-tours philosophy is well written: "Tailor Made Travel. Expertly Yours." + 3-step "Tell us what excites you -> we draft -> you refine."
- Named experts exist (Zoya Nair, Arjun Mehta).

What lost me (the core problems):
- "Tailormade" = rack of FIXED packages. Every real private-tour (e.g. Spain & Portugal Signature Journey) is a locked itinerary with a HARD per-person price (₹2,17,437) and "Book Now". No "make this mine / use as starting point". Triggers my package-padding fear directly.
- No real "Design My Holiday" flow. Closest is a Duration/Region/Country/Start-date filter + "Show me tours" — a catalogue filter, asks logistics not ME (pace, occasion, food/design interests). The 3-step copy promises a conversation the UI never delivers.
- "Loading..." empty states broke the spell twice: Japan trips list never populated, and testimonials showed "Loading latest stories...". Peak-desire moments handed me a spinner.
- Named designers are names only — Arjun Mehta no photo/voice; Zoya Nair "3 years of expertise" reads junior for a ₹5L+/pp trip.
- Japan never met my specific desire — no Cherry Blossom / Honeymoon / Food & Design / ryokan trip styles. Generic country page + "VIEW ALL TOURS".

Verdict: voice proves taste exists, but bespoke collapses into priced fixed packages and key pages still spin "Loading". Would click "Talk to an Expert" out of curiosity, not start a flow. Wouldn't book today.

Tech note: Next.js SPA; nav doors = International Immersions / Indian Getaways / All Inclusive Vacations (=/group-tours) / Tailormade Tours (=/private-tours).
