Reviewed the Cox & Kings homepage (http://localhost:5173/) via full-page + interaction screenshots.

**What won me (keep):**
- Editorial serif hero "More than a destination — it's a *journey*" with italic coral accent; cinematic Dolomites (Lago di Braies) hero. Genuinely premium first impression.
- "Design It Around You" bespoke path + "Help Me Decide" as a light first step (the 4-path "How would you like to travel?" section).
- Named specialists with faces + years ("Curated by Arjun Rao · Japan & East Asia specialist · 12 years") — biggest trust signal.
- Review copy that disarms my core anxiety: Priya Nair "Not a package — genuinely ours."
- "Watch & wander" Instagram/Shorts-style reels (Kyoto 241K views) + vertical player — my native dreaming mode.
- Cherry Blossom Japan card copy ("Tokyo neon to Kyoto temple gardens, timed to the petals") — the voice the whole site should use.

**What lost me (ranked craft flaws):**
1. "Specialists, not salespeople" collage: floating photos OVERLAP and obscure the body copy (desktop + mobile). Looks broken; reads as sloppy taste. Top fix.
2. Hero defaults to "Group Tour" + "from ₹95,000" — anchors group/cheap, wrong audience for a ₹5L bespoke buyer.
3. Prices stamped on every destination + journey card = catalogue/OTA energy; kills desire-before-price.
4. A few stock-feeling images (heritage backpacker) undercut the good photography.
5. "Japan for First-Timers" framing not aimed at well-travelled me.
6. Final CTA leans phone/WhatsApp/callback rather than an elegant "design my holiday" questionnaire as the closer.

**Why:** I judge trip quality by site craft. Bespoke must feel led by taste + a named human, not a package with a price tag.

**How to apply:** Single highest-impact change = make "Design It Around You" the hero of the experience (lead bespoke, not group/price) and turn it into a smart short flow (where/who/trip-style/what-matters) ending with a named specialist + sample itinerary. Earn details with curation, not a callback button.

Scores: Taste 7.5 · Bespoke 7 · Inspires 8 · Clarity 7 · Confidence-to-enquire 7. See related [[luxe2-homepage]].
