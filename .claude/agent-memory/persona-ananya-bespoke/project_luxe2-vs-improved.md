Compared the two luxury homepage variants for the panel report.

**What won me (both):** hero "Discover the World in Luxury & Style" with editorial italic serif over dark mountain + "SINCE 1758" kicker; the "Crafting unforgettable journeys since 1758" Jaipur block with floating Bespoke card and 267/100+/98% + Condé Nast proof; the "Don't choose a destination. Choose a feeling." section ("Slow mornings, private dinners, and nobody in the world but the two of you") — the single most on-brand-for-me moment; photo-first "Real journeys, real travellers" reviews; restrained "As featured in" press row (Forbes, Bazaar, Telegraph, CN).

**What /luxe2-improved adds that I value:** the "Two ways to travel" Private & Bespoke vs Escorted Group split (answers my #1 anxiety — bespoke or package?); "Every trip above is a starting point" reframing cards as customisable inspiration; a NAMED travel designer (Aanya Kapoor) with face + Call/WhatsApp — original had NO human at all; trust strip (24/7 support, financially protected).

**My verdict:** Prefer /luxe2-improved on INTENT/conversion, though original is calmer/more magazine-beautiful. Taste makes me scroll; the bespoke split + named human make me act.

**Persistent pain points (both):** strike-through "from ₹X" price chips on bespoke cards (e.g. Aman) read pure OTA, cheapen luxury, suggest padded quotes — keep per-person price for ESCORTED side only. "ENQUIRE" button everywhere is an OTA reflex word. No light "Design My Holiday" guided-question flow — only "Speak to an expert"/"Enquire" which ask too much too soon. "Choose a feeling" (my favourite) buried at the very bottom — should be near hero as the first step. Improved swapped review trip-photos for face close-ups (worse — I want places, not portraits). Improved desktop is long/repetitive, loses editorial calm. Mobile card grids read most like a booking portal.

**Why:** I'm the taste-first bespoke buyer; price-slash tone and "Enquire" forms break the premium spell that earns trust.
**How to apply:** When reviewing C&K luxury pages, push for: no strike-through pricing on bespoke, a feeling-led guided flow as the first step, named designer surfaced early, trip imagery (not faces) in reviews.
