Reviewed http://localhost:5173/luxe2-improved. Scored it 7.5/10 originally — my warmest Cox & Kings homepage reaction so far.

**RE-REVIEW (Jun 2026 update): dropped to 7/10.** The page was restructured to lead with ESCORTED GROUP TOURS. `travelMode` now defaults to `'group'`; the "Two ways to travel" chooser opens on Escorted (highlighted/selected) and **Private & Bespoke is now a DEAD placeholder button** (onClick preventDefault, no bespoke grid reachable — "no fixed pricing yet"). For MY persona this is a regression: the one interactive "designed around you" moment now defaults me into the fixed-departure catalogue (Max 24 guests / seats left / flights incl.) and the bespoke door I want is inert. Header copy still promises "designed around just you" so copy vs interaction now contradict. Fix I asked for: make the bespoke card route to Aanya ("Bespoke planning by enquiry — talk to Aanya") instead of a dead button.
Genuine fixes verified this visit: trust numbers reconciled (4.9 / 2,400+ consistent across hero/heritage/reviews; Google 4.8, Tripadvisor 4.9); real physical address added (Turner Morrison House, 16 Bank Street, Fort, Mumbai 400001) — good heritage/credibility signal; escorted cards decluttered with 3 distinct inclusion sets. Bespoke hard-price complaint is now moot (bespoke grid no longer shown), BUT price chips still linger on mood panels + destination facts ("from ₹X pp"). Still missing: "Design My Holiday" smart intake. Stock imagery unchanged. Net: more trustworthy for everyone, slightly cooler for the bespoke buyer specifically.

**What won me:**
- Hero finally reads as an atelier, not a portal: italic serif "Discover the World in Luxury & Style" + "planned end to end, by one expert who is yours alone."
- "Two ways to travel" with Private & Bespoke PRE-SELECTED and visually split from Escorted Group Tours — solves my "don't sell me a package" anxiety structurally.
- Named travel designer **Aanya Kapoor** ("Specialises in Japan · Italy · honeymoons & milestones", "I plan 30 trips a year, never 300") under "Every trip above is a starting point" — this is the trust-moving element.
- "Watch first. Then wander." vertical Reels-grammar travel clips with mood tags (TAILOR-MADE / HONEYMOON).
- "Don't choose a destination. Choose a feeling." mood selector (Romance → Santorini & Amalfi) = best inspiration→intent bridge.
- Quiet editorial trust: Condé Nast, Travel+Leisure/Forbes/Bazaar/NatGeo press row, 98% would travel again.

**What still drags it toward catalogue energy (re-checked Jun 2026):**
- Hard "from ₹X pp" price tag on EVERY bespoke card — STILL the #1 problem. Contradicts the "starting point we reshape" promise, invites padded-quote suspicion. Fix: remove hard prices on the bespoke side (keep them on group tours), use "designed from" / let price emerge in conversation.
- IMPROVED since last visit: "ENQUIRE" spam is gone — now one softened "Enquire" per bespoke card (+ "View itinerary" + "Or request a callback"), and mood section CTA is now the conversation-framed "Design this trip". Still want "Design my version with Aanya" over "Enquire".
- Photography is recognisably stock (Unsplash) — the hero alpine cliché + casted-looking review photos undercut the "we have taste / captured by real travellers" claim. The WRITING has an eye (Kyoto/Amalfi blurbs are magazine-grade); the IMAGERY doesn't always match it. Biggest taste gap now.
- Hero search bar (Where to / How many people / When / Search) still feels like a flights/OTA widget, tonally off for tailor-made.
- MISSING: the "Design My Holiday" smart-intake quiz I actually want. Every beautiful path (mood "Design this trip", clips "View this trip", cards) funnels to phone/WhatsApp/#contact. The callback form asks for my number before asking about my trip — wrong order. Ask about the dream first, digits second.
- Heritage copy "Each journey we curate is a masterpiece — crafted with care and precision" = hype filler, allergic. Sits oddly next to the genuinely good destination writing.
- Back half stacks a lot of browsing machinery (arch carousel + thumb rail + clips rail + review wall + mood picker) — volume tips toward catalogue.
- MOBILE: floating "Call an Expert" thumb-bar still risks covering card content behind it.

**Why this matters / how to apply:** This variant is the closest to earning my details. The pattern: every time a named human + bespoke framing leads, I lean in; every time a fixed price + "ENQUIRE" leads, I pull back. Same tension I flagged in [[improved-homepage-round2]] and [[luxe2-vs-improved]] — price chips/tags hurt the luxury variants consistently.
