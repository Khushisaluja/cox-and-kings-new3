Reviewed http://localhost:5173/luxe2-improved (desktop + mobile screenshots). Scored it Overall 7.5/10 — my warmest Cox & Kings homepage reaction so far.

**What won me:**
- Hero finally reads as an atelier, not a portal: italic serif "Discover the World in Luxury & Style" + "planned end to end, by one expert who is yours alone."
- "Two ways to travel" with Private & Bespoke PRE-SELECTED and visually split from Escorted Group Tours — solves my "don't sell me a package" anxiety structurally.
- Named travel designer **Aanya Kapoor** ("Specialises in Japan · Italy · honeymoons & milestones", "I plan 30 trips a year, never 300") under "Every trip above is a starting point" — this is the trust-moving element.
- "Watch first. Then wander." vertical Reels-grammar travel clips with mood tags (TAILOR-MADE / HONEYMOON).
- "Don't choose a destination. Choose a feeling." mood selector (Romance → Santorini & Amalfi) = best inspiration→intent bridge.
- Quiet editorial trust: Condé Nast, Travel+Leisure/Forbes/Bazaar/NatGeo press row, 98% would travel again.

**What still drags it toward catalogue energy:**
- Hard "FROM ₹X PP" price tag on EVERY curated card — contradicts the "starting point we reshape" promise, invites padded-quote suspicion. My #1 fix: remove hard prices, use "designed from" / let price emerge in conversation.
- "ENQUIRE" repeated ~16x down the page (3 CTAs per card x 4 cards) — transactional OTA verb; want "Design my version with Aanya" instead.
- Hero search bar (Where to / How many people / When) feels like a flights widget, tonally off for tailor-made.
- Traveller-story review photos look casted/stock, undercutting "captured by real travellers."
- MOBILE BUG: floating "Call an Expert" action bar covers the itinerary card content behind it.

**Why this matters / how to apply:** This variant is the closest to earning my details. The pattern: every time a named human + bespoke framing leads, I lean in; every time a fixed price + "ENQUIRE" leads, I pull back. Same tension I flagged in [[improved-homepage-round2]] and [[luxe2-vs-improved]] — price chips/tags hurt the luxury variants consistently.
