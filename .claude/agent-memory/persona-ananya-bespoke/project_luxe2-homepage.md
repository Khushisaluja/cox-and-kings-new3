Reviewed the /luxe2 luxury homepage (navy palette, sticky nav turns blue on scroll).

**What won me (keep doing):**
- Hero "Discover the World in Luxury & Style" — italic serif + sans pairing reads like a real travel brand.
- "Since 1758" heritage section with stats + press logos (Nat Geo, T+L, Forbes, Telegraph) = good trust proof.
- Santorini arch-framed destination image — restraint, whitespace, taste.
- Travel Clips reels + full-screen clip viewer ("Cherry blossom dawn at Kiyomizu-dera... before-the-crowds access... DESIGN THIS TRIP", TAILOR-MADE tag) — the strongest moment; copy sounds like a human with taste.
- "Don't choose a destination. Choose a feeling." mood picker + "Slow mornings, private dinners, nobody but the two of you." — nails the anniversary brief.
- Final line "Your next journey deserves a quarter-millennium of judgment" — strong.

**What lost me (drop-off, ranked):**
1. "Our Curations" stay cards with bold hard prices (₹62k, ₹1.15L) + double CTAs (View Itinerary + Enquire) = catalogue/portal energy, reads like fixed packages. Biggest trust-killer, sits too high on page.
2. Orange CTAs (SPEAK TO AN EXPERT, Ask Enaya FAB) cheapen an otherwise premium navy page — OTA/urgency colour.
3. No "how bespoke works" explanation, no "this is a starting point, we'll design your version" line — leaves bespoke-vs-package ambiguous.
4. No named travel designer / human face; no mention of 24/7 on-trip support.
5. Every path funnels to Enquire / Speak to an Expert — feels like lead-gen, not a light intelligent first step.

**Why:** As a taste-first bespoke buyer, hard price tags on photos reclassify the brand from atelier to portal; orange signals discount.
**How to apply:** When reviewing C&K luxury pages, reward editorial clips/mood/feeling sections; flag priced package grids, orange CTAs, missing "how it works", and missing named designer.
