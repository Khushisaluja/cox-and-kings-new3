Reviewed the localhost:4173 luxury landing page (2nd pass, after team fixes). Scored 7/10.

What won me:
- Four-door "How would you like to travel?" section: "Design it around you / Start Designing" is the bespoke door + light first step I always want. Plus "Help me decide" and "Just talk to someone, no scripts".
- Named, credentialed designers on curation cards (e.g. "Curated by Arjun Rao · Japan & East Asia specialist · 12 years", with face). Real proof of a human with taste.
- "designed from ₹X / person" wording softens the package feeling (a fix from prior rounds).
- Hero serif type with peach italic on "journey" feels expensive.
- Clips section (Instagram-native traveller films) and bespoke testimonial ("Not a package — genuinely ours").
- Tasteful trust block (20% deposit, free cancellation, visa rebook/refund, secure payments never to personal account).

What still cools me (the through-line of my objection):
- Page's center of gravity is ESCORTED GROUP TOUR. "Begin with a little desire" block is a group spec sheet (Max 18, tour manager, "why we cost more / capped at 18 not a 40-seat coach"). 3 of 4 curation cards are group/family; only 1 (Japan, Bespoke Private) is mine.
- "Paced for comfort as standard — lifts in every hotel, senior-friendly days" reads as a retiree/group default; tells me I'm the exception here.
- Specialists scattered-photo collage reads broken/colliding-with-text, not art-directed (in static shot).
- Heritage "Bespoke itineraries" card has low-contrast white text over photo.
- Destination grid still per-tile "from ₹X / person" = catalogue energy.
- Mobile: two floating elements (orange Call-an-Expert bar + Ask Enaya bubble).
- Closing CTA = phone/WhatsApp/callback only; wanted a "Design my holiday" door there. "quarter-millennium of judgment" copy lands cold.

**Why:** consistent across my reviews — I reward a real bespoke door + named designer, but penalize group-tour gravity and anything that makes a private couple feel like the exception.
**How to apply:** if re-reviewed, check whether private/couples self-select earlier and whether the bespoke lane stops being outnumbered by group cards. Related: [[improved-homepage-round2]], [[luxe2-improved]].
