Reviewed `/about-us1` ("The Ledger" concept) on 2026-07-13. Highest aesthetic mark I've given Cox & Kings: **8/10 taste, 5/10 trust-to-design-my-trip.**

**What's genuinely good** — Cormorant Garamond + Work Sans, navy/sienna/paper. Oldstyle figures in the big year readout. Roman-numeral folio seals; the struck-through XII on 2020 is the single best detail on the whole site. No photography on the timeline reads as restraint, not absence. Does not look like a template.

**The fatal bug (verified with Playwright at every scroll position):** the blank final ledger entry — "the next entry hasn't been written" and its CTA `START IT WITH A SPECIALIST` — is authored in the DOM but **never reaches the screen**. The horizontal track ends before it scrolls in. So a 12,800px pinned scrub of 268 years terminates on **2020, the insolvency**, then dumps into a paper section. The concept's entire payoff is unreachable.

**Pacing is inverted:** 1758→1911 runs ~22px/year (correct — the intentional empty century is fine). 1930→2008 runs ~100px/year, so the *slowest, emptiest* stretch is the 1980s–2000s where nothing happens (~4,500px of dead air between the 1970s entry and 2018). "SKIP THE HISTORY" existing is an admission the section is too long.

**Strategic miss:** 34 trust/failure words vs ~5 words of desire across 1,776 words. The page is written for Sunita (reassurance), not me. The ONLY sentence that made me want to book was Arjun Rao's: *"Books the ryokan, then calls ahead about the dietary requirement you mentioned in passing."* That one line outperforms the entire timeline — and it's buried at 15px in a three-up of **initials in circles, not faces**.

**Why:** Restraint about the *past* is confidence. Restraint about the *present* (no faces, no hotels, no trips) is evasion.

**How to apply:** If asked to re-review this page or a variant — check first whether the blank final entry actually lands on screen, whether the specialists have real photographs, and whether any desire copy has been added. Those three are the difference between 5/10 and 8/10 on trust. Related: [[live-coxandkings-com]], [[luxe2-improved]].
