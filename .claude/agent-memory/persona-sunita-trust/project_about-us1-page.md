The `/about-us1` page exists specifically to rebuild legacy trust after the 2020 collapse. Reviewed 2026-07-13.

**What it gets right (do not regress these):**
- Names the 2019 default, 2020 NCLT insolvency, and that "many were never made whole" in plain words: "we are not going to call it anything softer than that." This *earns* trust with the cautious-senior segment. Softening it would backfire.
- FAQ "I'm owed money from a booking made before 2020" answers honestly and adds "we will not try to sell you a holiday while you are on the phone." Strongest line on the page.
- Phone `+91 8556001700` (tel:) in header, hero, mid-page, contact and footer; WhatsApp `wa.me/918556001700`. Mobile has a sticky "Talk to a specialist" call bar.
- Senior-care copy is genuinely good: pace/stairs stated honestly, veg/Jain food pre-arranged, "Travelling with parents", 24h WhatsApp line.

**Confirmed trust gaps (as of this review):**
- **No physical office address, registered entity name, CIN, or GSTIN anywhere on the page.** Words "address", "office", "registered", "Ltd", "Pvt", "Delhi", "Mumbai" do not appear. Biggest single miss for a walk-in-office-seeking senior.
- **Words "refund", "cancellation", "escrow", "insurance" appear nowhere** — on a page whose entire subject is people who lost deposits.
- Section 04 tells the reader to "ask where your deposit is held, and who is holding it" — but the page never answers that question itself. Reads as a dodge.
- **Zero photographs of any human.** Only 3 `<img>` on the page (2 logos + a crest). Specialists are initials in circles (MS/AR/NV), no faces. No reviews/testimonials at all.
- Duplicate-looking logo top-left: `cox-logo-new.png` at x=32 and `cox-crest-new.png` at x=132 sit side by side, reading as a broken/doubled logo (desktop and mobile).
- "SKIP THE HISTORY" button exists and is visible, but lands at scrollY ~11521 while the timeline runs to ~12070 — i.e. still inside the pinned section, in a half-cut-off dead zone with chopped text. Escape hatch feels broken.
- Page is ~19.7 screens tall; the pinned timeline alone is ~9.1 screens.
- Heavy small type: 14 elements at 10px, 9 at 11px, 12+ at 12px.
- Mobile header shows only a hamburger — no visible phone number (sticky bottom bar mitigates).
- Owner "Wilson & Hughes Pte Ltd, Singapore … also hold businesses in supply chain and building materials" — stated but unproven; raises as many questions as it answers.

**Why:** This page is the primary trust artefact for the Legacy-Reassurance segment; its stated promise is "how to check us before you trust us with a rupee."
**How to apply:** Judge future revisions against whether they close the *verifiability* gaps above — the honesty is already there, the checkable proof is not.
